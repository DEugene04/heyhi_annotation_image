The equation of a circle is \(x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 6y - 77 = 0\).
(a) Find the x-coordinates of the points \(A\) and \(B\) where the circle intersects the x-axis.                     [2]
(b) Find the point of intersection of the tangents to the circle at \(A\) and \(B\).                             [6]
(i)
\(x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 6y - 77 = 0\)
put \(y = 0\)
\(x^2 + (0)^2 - 4x + 6(0) - 77 = 0\)
\(x^2 - 4x - 77 = 0\)
\(x^2 - 11x + 7x - 77 = 0\)
\((x - 11)(x + 7) = 0\)
\(x = 11, x = -7\)
(ii)
\(x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 6y - 77 = 0\)
\(x^2 - 4x + (2)^2 - (2)^2 + y^2 - 6y + (3)^2 - (3)^2 = 77\)
