I love honest conversations with myself. I talk to
myself a lot- I reflect a lot. And I think this
act is essential because at some point you will
realise that you are discovering happiness by doing,
so. Mentally talking about the worldly attachments
and how courageously you stand up for what matters,
the struggles you have grown through, the steps you
are taking. Sometimes I find myself just contemplating
over things and it makes me happy because I am able
to self-respect.
Oh how important self-reflections to me is, because I
need to know that I am not always right. I am
happy when I am able to know that a particular
instance happened to me because of this and that.
Because you see, not everyone can sit down and chat
with themselves. So choose to chat with yourself
some time, it's a living, breathing thing, an energy
within.
- Sanjida
