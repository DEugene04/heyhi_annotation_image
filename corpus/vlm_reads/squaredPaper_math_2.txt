a) \(\log_2 3 + \log_2 x = 2\)
\(\Leftrightarrow\)
\(\log_2 3x = \log_2 2^2\)
\(\Leftrightarrow\) \(3x = 2^2\)
\(3x = 4\)
\(x = \frac{4}{3}\)
b) \(\log_6 12 - \log_6 x = 3\)
\(\log_6 \frac{12}{x} = 3\)
\(\log_6 \frac{12}{x} = \log_6 6^3\)
\(\frac{12}{x} = 6^3\)
\(\frac{12}{6^3} = x = \frac{12}{216} = \frac{1}{18}\)
c) \(2 \log_5 x = 1 + \log_5 6\)
\(\log_5 x^2 = \log_5 5 + \log_5 6\)
\(\log_5 x^2 = \log_5 5 \cdot 6\)
\(\log_5 x^2 = \log_5 30\)
\(\Leftrightarrow x = \sqrt{30}\)
