[text   ] (2w) Solve for \( x \) :
[math   ] (1w) \[
\frac{(x+2)!}{x!}-\frac{(x+1)!}{(x-1)!}=16
\]
[text   ] (3w) Solution
[math   ] (1w) x!=x(x-1)(x-2) \ldots \ldots
[math   ] (1w) (x+2)!=(x+2)(x+1)(x!)
[math   ] (1w) (x+1)!=(x+1)(x)(x-1)!
[math   ] (1w) \frac{(x+2)(x+1)(x+1)}{x+}-\frac{(x+1)(x)(x-1)}{(x-1)!}=16
[math   ] (1w) (x+2)(x+1)-(x+1)(x)=16
[math   ] (1w) x^{2}+3 x+2 \times x^{2}-x=16
[math   ] (1w) 2 x=16-2
[math   ] (0w) 2 x=14, x=7
